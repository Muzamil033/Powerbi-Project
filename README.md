# Powerbi-Project
# Welcome to my project

📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides interactive insights into sales revenue, total orders, product performance, city-wise sales, and category-wise revenue.

The main objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights that help understand sales trends and overall business performance.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.

Track total orders and total revenue.

Identify top-performing cities based on revenue.

Analyze revenue generated by different product categories.

Identify the best-performing products.

Compare product prices with generated revenue.

Build an interactive dashboard with filters and visualizations.

📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

Revenue by City: Compares total revenue across Chennai, Kolkata, Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore.

Top Selling Products: Displays product-wise revenue performance.

Product Count by City: Compares product quantities across different cities.

Revenue by Category: Shows the revenue contribution of Clothing, Electronics, and Groceries.

Price vs Revenue: Analyzes the relationship between product prices and generated revenue.

Interactive Filters: Allows users to filter the dashboard by category, year, quarter, month, and day.

🔍 Key Business Insights

Chennai generated the highest revenue among the five cities, with 1,425,707.

Clothing was the highest revenue-generating category, contributing 2,118,892.

Phones were the top-performing products by revenue, generating 837,621.

Clothing contributed 34.41% of total revenue.

Electronics contributed 33.52% of total revenue.

Groceries contributed 32.07% of total revenue.

The dataset contains 1,000 orders across 5 cities and 3 product categories.
